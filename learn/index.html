Straight answers

Are these the real exam questions?

No. They are original CertShield questions, written from each vendor’s published exam guide and checked against its current documentation. We do not use or publish real exam content.

Does a good score mean I will pass?

No tool can promise that. Many exams report a scaled score, or only pass or fail, so a practice percentage cannot be turned into a pass mark. We show the range your score supports and which topics to work on. Read the method.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no payment and no advertising on these pages.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser. The mock exam makes no network request while you take it. Read the privacy page.

Who made this?

CertShield, an independent publisher of certification study material, founded by Priya Dwivedi. It is not affiliated with any certification owner. About this site.

How are the trap types decided?

A CertShield editor read each question’s explanation and chose its main trap type. It is a judgement, not a measurement. How it works.
