Missed requirement

It works, but it breaks a condition in the question.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 192 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 15% of all questions
  • 3rd most common of 9

What it is

The option would work in general, but the scenario set a condition and it breaks it: a cost cap, a latency target, without code changes, automatically, for every account. Or it solves one half of a two-part need.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • The stem contains a qualifier such as least, fastest, without, only, automatically or regardless.
  • Two requirements are listed or implied.
  • An option is sound engineering but leaves the team with work the stem ruled out.

How to beat it

  1. Underline every condition in the stem before you read the options.
  2. Check each option against every condition, not only the main goal.
  3. In choose-two questions, make sure each pick answers a different requirement.

A quick example

A team has no operations staff and wants the least effort. One option is technically excellent but needs servers to be patched and scaled by hand. It is good engineering and the wrong answer.
