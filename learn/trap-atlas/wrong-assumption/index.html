Wrong assumption

A default or limit that is not actually true.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 340 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 26% of all questions
  • 1st most common of 9

What it is

You assume a default, limit or behaviour that is not true: that a managed service is highly available out of the box, that a feature does something it does not, or that a confident claim is correct without checking it.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • An option rests on a sentence that says always, automatically or by default.
  • The claim sounds plausible, but you cannot say where you learned it.
  • A limitation is asserted in order to rule out the best answer.

How to beat it

  1. Recall the documented behaviour, not what seems logical.
  2. Treat a confident claim about a limitation with suspicion, and test it against what you know.
  3. Notice which of your assumptions the stem has not confirmed.

A quick example

A candidate assumes a managed database fails over between zones by default. Many managed databases only do so once a high-availability setting has been switched on.
