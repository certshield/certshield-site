Look-alike option

Related, but it does a different job.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 297 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 23% of all questions
  • 2nd most common of 9

What it is

Every option is a real thing, and one of them sounds right. It is related to the scenario but does a different job: a similar service, a neighbouring feature, or a term that differs by one word.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • Most options are legitimate in general; none is absurd.
  • The stem's key phrase points at one specific capability or definition.
  • Two options share a word or a purpose and differ in what they actually do.

How to beat it

  1. Say in a few words what each option actually does.
  2. Find the phrase in the stem that only one of them can satisfy.
  3. If two options overlap, look for the detail that separates them: scope, timing, direction or audience.

A quick example

A scenario needs events delivered to many independent consumers. One option is a queue, where each message goes to a single consumer. Another is a publish-and-subscribe topic. Both move messages, but only one fits the words many independent consumers.
