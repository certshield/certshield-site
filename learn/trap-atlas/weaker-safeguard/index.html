Weaker safeguard

It reports or advises, when the question needs it enforced.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 79 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 6% of all questions
  • 6th most common of 9

What it is

You choose a control that reports, advises or is simply convenient, when the scenario needs one that enforces. A detective control instead of a preventive one, a policy or a prompt instead of a technical mechanism, or a promise instead of a guarantee.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • The stem says must never, cannot, regardless of or even if.
  • An option relies on people, or on a model, behaving well.
  • An option only logs, alerts, reviews or documents.

How to beat it

  1. Ask whether the control still works if someone ignores it.
  2. Match the verb: stop it from happening needs prevention; find out when it happens needs detection.
  3. Prefer structural limits over instructions.

A quick example

The requirement says a setting must never be changed by any administrator. A weekly report that lists changes detects them afterwards. Only a control that sits outside the administrators' reach prevents them.

A made-up illustration of the pattern. It is not a question from any mock or real exam.
