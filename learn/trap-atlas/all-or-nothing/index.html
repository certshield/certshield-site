All-or-nothing answer

A blanket or drastic response where a targeted one is right.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 64 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 5% of all questions
  • 7th most common of 9

What it is

You pick an extreme, blanket or drastic response: shut everything down, grant everything, treat every case the same, or never change anything. The right answer is targeted, proportionate or conditional.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • Options with words such as always, never, all, every or completely.
  • The stem asks to keep something working while another thing is fixed.
  • One option is dramatic and another is measured.

How to beat it

  1. Look for the option that changes only what the stem names.
  2. Ask what normal work must keep running.
  3. Remember that one fixed rule rarely suits two different workloads.
