Familiar-name reflex

A keyword triggers the answer you know best.

Practise against itAll nine traps
  • 168 of 1,310 mock exam questions
  • 13% of all questions
  • 4th most common of 9

What it is

A word in the question sets off the product or technique you know best, and you answer before checking that it fits.

How to spot it, and how to beat it

How to spot it

  • The first answer that comes to mind is the best-known one.
  • The stem mentions a word that matches a famous product's marketing, such as global, real time, AI or secure.
  • You feel certain within a second.

How to beat it

  1. Name the second-best option and say why it loses.
  2. Ask which sentence of the stem rules out your first choice.
  3. Treat strong familiarity as a reason to slow down, not to speed up.

A quick example

A stem says global users and fast. The reflex answer is a content delivery network. The stem also says the traffic cannot be cached, which is exactly why that answer fails.
