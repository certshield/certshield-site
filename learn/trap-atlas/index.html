Trap Atlas

The Trap Atlas: nine ways certification questions mislead you

Wrong answers on a certification exam are rarely random: they are built to look right. We read the explanation of 1,310 mock exam questions and sorted each one by the main way it tries to mislead you. Learn the nine and you will start to see them coming.

How common is each one?

Each question has one main trap, so every question is counted once. Out of 1,310 mock exam questions:

  • Wrong assumption340 · 26%
  • Look-alike option297 · 23%
  • Missed requirement192 · 15%
  • Familiar-name reflex168 · 13%
  • Symptom, not cause100 · 8%
  • Weaker safeguard79 · 6%
  • All-or-nothing answer64 · 5%
  • Heavier than needed45 · 3%
  • Exact detail to remember25 · 2%

Shares are rounded to whole percent, so they may not add up to exactly 100%. They describe CertShield’s mock exams, not the real exams, whose questions are not public.

How the atlas was made

Every mock exam question comes with an Exam Trap note explaining how its wrong answers mislead. We read each of those notes and gave the question one main trap type: the one that best explains the mistake most candidates would make. Many questions carry more than one trap; the atlas records the main one.

This is editorial judgement, not a measurement, so two reviewers could disagree about a few questions. If a tag looks wrong to you, tell us with the Feedback button. Read exactly how it works.
