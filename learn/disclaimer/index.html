Independence and disclaimer

CertShield’s mock exams are independent, original and a study aid, not a guarantee. Read what that means.

Independent preparation

CertShield is an independent publisher of study material. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any certification owner or exam provider mentioned on this site. Vendor, product and certification names belong to their owners and are used only to say which exam a mock exam prepares for.

Not the real exam

Mock exam questions are original CertShield content. They are not real exam questions, not “exam dumps”, and not copied from any exam. A mock exam result, a range or an Error Signature is a study aid; it is not a guarantee that you will pass.

Accuracy and corrections

Exam facts such as question counts, time limits, fees and weights change, so always confirm them with the exam provider before you book. Each mock exam shows the date it was last checked. If you find a mistake, tell us with the Feedback button and we will correct it. The exam library keeps a public corrections log ↗.
