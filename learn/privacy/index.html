Your data

Privacy

What these free mock exams store in your browser, what they never collect, and how optional analytics works.

The short version

These pages have no accounts and no sign-up, and ask for no email address. A mock exam runs in your browser: your answers, confidence marks and results stay on your device and are never sent to us. Google Analytics, the only tracking tool, loads only if you choose “Allow analytics”.

What is stored in your browser

  • An unfinished mock exam (certshield.assessment.v2.<exam>): your answers, confidence marks, flags, your place and, if timed, the time left, so you can resume.
  • A short history (certshield.assessment.history.v2.<exam>): the score, accuracy and date of your last three finished attempts at each exam.
  • Study mode (certshield.study.v1.<exam>): your place and answers in a study session.
  • My progress (certshield.learn.progress.v1): a summary of each finished mock exam (score, range, result by topic and trap type), up to five per exam, for the My progress page.
  • Your analytics choice (certshield.consent.v1).
  • An unsent feedback draft (certshield.feedback.draft), kept for this browser tab only (session storage) and deleted when you send it or close the tab.

All of this stays on your own device. Clear it in your browser settings, or use My progress to download or delete it. The start-over option inside a mock exam clears that exam’s unfinished attempt.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose “Allow analytics”, we load Google Analytics 4. It records standard visit data: pages viewed, links clicked, referring site, approximate location, device and browser type. When you start or finish a mock exam it also records the event, the mode (mock exam or study) and the exam’s name. It never receives your answers, confidence marks or results. Google acts as our service provider; see Google’s privacy policy.

If you choose “No thanks”, nothing from Google is loaded. You can change your mind at any time with Analytics settings in the footer; choosing “No thanks” stops the tag and removes its cookies.

Feedback box (optional)

The Feedback button lets you write to us. Nothing is sent until you press “Send feedback”. We then receive what you wrote, the address and title of the page (and, in a mock exam, the question number you were on) and your browser’s language. There is no email field on these pages, so we cannot reply; please do not put personal details in the message. The message travels over an encrypted connection to certshield.academy, which passes it to our email provider, Brevo, for delivery to contact@certshield.academy. A Cloudflare Turnstile check, loaded only when you open the box (never on page load), confirms you are not a bot; it is covered by Cloudflare’s own privacy policy. We keep no copy on this website.

Links to other sites

Explanations link to the vendors’ official documentation, and pages link to the CertShield exam library, which has its own privacy page. Once you leave this site, the other site’s policies apply.

Children

These pages are a study aid for certification candidates and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights and contact

Apart from feedback you choose to send us, we hold no personal data about you on this site, so there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete here. For a privacy question, including about data in Google Analytics if you allowed it, email contact@certshield.academy. This page is written in plain language and describes how the site works today; it is not legal advice. Last updated 19 October 2026.
