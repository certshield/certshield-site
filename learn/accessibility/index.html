Accessibility

Accessibility statement

How these free mock exams aim to work for everyone, how we check, and what to do if something does not work for you.

Our aim

We want these pages to work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, a phone, a large font or a high-contrast setting. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we do

  • Every page has a skip link, one main heading, a clear heading order and labelled landmarks.
  • Everything can be done from the keyboard, including answering questions, moving between them and flagging them, with a visible focus outline.
  • Charts and range bars always come with the same numbers as text; no meaning depends on colour alone.
  • Tables use header cells, and a wide table scrolls inside a region you can reach with the keyboard.
  • Text stays readable when you zoom to 200%, and pages reflow to a single column on a phone without sideways scrolling.
  • Animations are switched off for people who ask their device for reduced motion.

How we check

Every kind of page in this section is run through the axe-core automated checker for WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules, and the mock exam is checked in each of its states: start, a question, the results and the answer review. Automated tools find only part of the problems, so please tell us about any that you meet.

Known limits

Explanations quote the vendors’ official documentation and link to it; those sites are not under our control. The timed mock exam has a countdown that cannot be paused once started; choose the untimed option if a timer is a barrier for you.

Tell us

If something here does not work for you, use the Feedback button or write to contact@certshield.academy and say what you were trying to do. Last reviewed 19 October 2026.
