About these free mock exams

Free certification mock exams that tell you the truth about your score.

What this is

43 free certification mock exams with 1,310 explained questions, an honest range for your score, a map of the traps that exams set, and a private record of your progress. There is no sign-up, no advertising and no email field, and everything runs in your browser.

Why it exists

Most practice tests give you a percentage and a pat on the back. That number is less certain than it looks, and it hides the tricks that questions use. We wanted a practice test that is honest about both.

How it is made

Every question is original CertShield content, written from the vendor’s published exam guide and checked against its current documentation. Each mock exam shows the date it was last checked. Topic weights come from the guide itself. Read the method for exactly how scores, ranges and trap tags are worked out.

Who is behind it

CertShield is an independent publisher of certification study material, founded by Priya Dwivedi. CertShield is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any certification owner. Read the independence statement.

The rest of the library

Exam facts, fees by country, comparisons and planning tools live in the CertShield exam library ↗. These mock exams link there and it links back.
