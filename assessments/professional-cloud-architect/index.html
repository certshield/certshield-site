About the Official Professional Cloud Architect Exam

Sourced directly from the official Professional Cloud Architect Exam Guide, published by Google Cloud — Live version effective October 30, 2025.

Questions
50‑60 multiple choice and multiple select questions, some referencing one of four fictitious case studies (Altostrat Media, Cymbal Retail, EHR Healthcare, KnightMotives Automotive) that together account for roughly 20‑30% of exam content
Time Limit
2 hours
Passing Score
Not publicly disclosed by Google Cloud (pass/fail result only)
Registration Fee
$200, plus tax where applicable
Delivery
Pearson VUE — online-proctored (remote) or onsite-proctored at an authorized test center
Validity
2 years from the date of passing, with a shorter 1-hour, 25-question renewal exam available (centered on a single case study aligned to generative AI)

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture ~25%
  • Managing and provisioning a cloud solution infrastructure ~17.5%
  • Designing for security and compliance ~17.5%
  • Analyzing and optimizing technical and business processes ~15%
  • Managing implementation ~12.5%
  • Ensuring solution and operations excellence ~12.5%

Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for professionals who leverage Google Cloud technologies to design, develop, and manage robust, secure, scalable, efficient, cost-effective, highly available, and flexible solutions that drive business objectives — proficient in enterprise cloud strategy, solution design, workload migration approaches, deployment and orchestration, optimization, architectural best practices, and the Google Cloud Well-Architected Framework. Recommended experience: 3+ years of industry experience, including 1+ years designing and managing solutions using Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese in addition to English. Some questions reference one of four fictitious business case studies for additional context; several of the case-study businesses use Google Cloud's generative AI solutions to solve their fictional challenges.
