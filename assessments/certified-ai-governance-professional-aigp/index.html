About the Official Certified AI Governance Professional (AIGP) Exam

Sourced directly from the official Certified AI Governance Professional (AIGP) Exam Guide, published by IAPP (International Association of Privacy Professionals) — Body of Knowledge v2.1, effective February 2, 2026.

Questions
100 items (85 scored, 15 unscored)
Time Limit
165 minutes, plus an optional 15-minute break
Passing Score
Scaled score of 300 on a 100‑500 scale
Registration Fee
$649 for IAPP members, $799 for nonmembers (first attempt)
Delivery
Online-proctored (Pearson OnVUE) or at a Pearson VUE test center
Validity
2 years, beginning the day after passing

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Understanding the foundations of AI governance 16‑20 items

Who this certification is for:

IAPP's own candidate handbook recommends this credential for professionals in AI compliance, risk management, legal and governance, data science, AI project management, model ops, social science, and trust roles — anyone responsible for adopting or managing AI in an organization.

There are no prerequisites for this credential; unscored questions do not count toward the final score.
