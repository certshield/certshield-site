Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for professionals responsible for the daily management of a Google Workspace environment — managing user accounts, configuring core Workspace services such as Gmail and Drive, and ensuring security and compliance of Workspace data — typically IT administrators, systems administrators, help desk personnel, technical support engineers, and collaboration engineers. Recommended experience: 6 months of Google Workspace Super Admin experience in a real or test environment, with hands-on experience on Business Plus edition and knowledge of Enterprise edition features.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
