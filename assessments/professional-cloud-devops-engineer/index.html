About the Official Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer Exam

Sourced directly from the official Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer Exam Guide, published by Google Cloud — Live version effective November 23, 2026.

Questions
50-60 multiple choice questions
Time Limit
2 hours
Passing Score
Not publicly disclosed by Google Cloud (pass/fail result only)
Registration Fee
$200, plus tax where applicable
Delivery
Pearson VUE — online-proctored (remote) or onsite-proctored at a test center
Validity
2 years from the date of passing, renewed by retaking the standard exam within the 60 days before expiration (Google lists no shorter renewal exam for this certification)

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Establishing and bootstrapping foundational infrastructure and organizational governance ~18%
  • Building, implementing, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, including continuous testing, for application, infrastructure, and ML workloads ~30%
  • Applying site reliability engineering practices ~18%
  • Implementing observability practices and troubleshooting issues ~22%
  • Optimizing performance and cost ~12%

Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for professionals who implement processes and capabilities throughout the systems development lifecycle using Google-recommended methodologies, tools, and agents, enable efficient software and infrastructure delivery while balancing reliability with delivery speed, and optimize, secure, and maintain production systems and services for both performance and cost. Recommended experience: 3+ years of industry experience, including 1+ years designing and managing production systems using Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites; also offered in Japanese in addition to English. The new exam guide took effect on November 23, 2026.
