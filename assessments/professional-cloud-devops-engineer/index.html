Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for professionals who implement processes and capabilities throughout the systems development lifecycle using Google-recommended methodologies and tools, enable efficient software and infrastructure delivery while balancing reliability with delivery speed, and optimize and maintain production systems and services for both performance and cost. Recommended experience: 3+ years of industry experience, including 1+ years designing and managing solutions using Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
