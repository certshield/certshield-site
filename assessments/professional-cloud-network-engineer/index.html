Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification page describes this credential for professionals responsible for the design, implementation, and management of Google Cloud network infrastructure — skilled in VPC, routing, load balancing, Cloud NAT, and Cloud DNS, with expertise in hybrid/multicloud connectivity and network security — typically with 3+ years of industry experience, including 1+ years working with Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites; also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
