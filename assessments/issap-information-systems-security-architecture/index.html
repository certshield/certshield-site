About the Official ISSAP Information Systems Security Architecture Exam

Sourced directly from the official ISSAP Information Systems Security Architecture Exam Guide, published by ISC2 — Live version effective August 1, 2025.

Questions
125 multiple-choice and advanced-item-type questions (scored using a scaled scoring model)
Time Limit
3 hours
Passing Score
700 out of 1000 points (scaled score; raw item weighting is not disclosed)
Registration Fee
$599 (Americas/APAC pricing; regional pricing varies, e.g., UK £479, EMEA €575.04)
Delivery
Pearson VUE — onsite-proctored at an authorized test center (not currently offered via OnVUE online proctoring)
Validity
3-year certification cycle. Candidates who hold an active CISSP sync ISSAP renewal to their CISSP cycle (60 CPE credits per 3-year term, no separate annual maintenance fee); candidates without a CISSP require 140 CPE credits per 3-year term plus a $125 annual maintenance fee ($75 if already holding another ISC2 certification)

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) ~21%
  • Security Architecture Modeling ~22%
  • Infrastructure and System Security ~32%
  • Identity and Access Management (IAM) Architecture ~25%

Who this certification is for:

ISC2 positions ISSAP as ideal for security leaders who specialize in designing security solutions and providing risk-based guidance to meet organizational goals — named example roles are System Architect, Chief Technology Officer, System and Network Designer, Business Analyst, and Chief Security Officer.

Requires either an active CISSP credential plus 2 years of full-time experience in one or more of the four ISSAP domains, or 7 years of cumulative full-time experience across two or more domains (one year may be waived with a relevant post-secondary degree or an approved ISC2 credential). The exam outline effective August 1, 2025 consolidated six legacy domains into four and added AI-related architecture content (e.g., securing AI workloads, identity for autonomous agents, prompt-injection monitoring). ANSI/ANAB accredited to ISO/IEC 17024 and approved under U.S. DoD Directive 8140.
