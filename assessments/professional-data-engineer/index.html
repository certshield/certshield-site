Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification page describes this credential for professional data engineers who collect, transform, store, and deliver data; design robust data infrastructure; evaluate and select solutions; and administer data platforms — typically with 3+ years of industry experience, including 1+ years working with Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites; also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
