Who this certification is for:

Databricks' own exam guide describes the ideal candidate as someone who can discover, query, and manage certified datasets in Unity Catalog, import data through multiple methods, and execute and optimize queries for data analysis; expected to complete basic data analytics tasks such as managing data, analyzing data, and building dashboards.

Unscored calibration questions may appear on the real exam; extra time is already factored in to account for them. The guide updated on October 8, 2026 renamed AI/BI Genie spaces to Genie Agents (Genie Spaces).
