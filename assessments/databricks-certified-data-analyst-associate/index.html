About the Official Databricks Certified Data Analyst Associate Exam

Per Databricks' own official Data Analyst Associate Exam Guide (covering the exam version live as of October 30, 2025): 45 scored multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes (unscored calibration questions may appear, with extra time already factored in to account for them), delivered online-proctored or at a test center, a $200 registration fee, and a credential valid for 2 years requiring a full retake to recertify — Databricks does not publish a specific passing-score percentage. The real exam is organized into the same 9 domains this diagnostic mirrors: Understanding of Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, Managing Data, Importing Data, Executing Queries Using Databricks SQL and Databricks SQL Warehouses, Analyzing Queries, Working with Dashboards and Visualizations in Databricks, Developing, Sharing, and Maintaining AI/BI Genie Spaces, Data Modeling with Databricks SQL, and Securing Data.
