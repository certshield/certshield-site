About the Official Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) Exam

Sourced directly from the official Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) Exam Guide, published by ISC2 — Live version effective April 15, 2024.

Questions
100–150 scored and unscored items (Computerized Adaptive Testing; the exam ends once the scoring engine reaches a statistically confident pass/fail determination, or at 150 items)
Time Limit
3 hours
Passing Score
700 out of 1000 points (scaled score; raw item weighting is not disclosed)
Registration Fee
$749 (Americas/APAC pricing; regional pricing varies by market)
Delivery
Pearson VUE — onsite-proctored at an authorized test center (not currently offered via OnVUE online proctoring); Computerized Adaptive Testing is used for all five available exam languages (English, Spanish, German, Japanese, and Chinese, the last on restricted quarterly windows)
Validity
3-year certification cycle requiring 120 CPE credits (at least 90 in Group A, domain-specific activities), plus a single $135 annual maintenance fee that covers any combination of ISC2 certifications held simultaneously

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Security and Risk Management ~16%
  • Asset Security ~10%
  • Security Architecture and Engineering ~13%
  • Communication and Network Security ~13%
  • Identity and Access Management (IAM) ~13%
  • Security Assessment and Testing ~12%
  • Security Operations ~13%
  • Software Development Security ~10%

Who this certification is for:

ISC2 describes CISSP as ideal for experienced security practitioners, managers, and executives who want to prove their knowledge across a wide array of security practices and principles — naming roles such as CISO, CIO, Director of Security, IT Director/Manager, Security Systems Engineer, Security Analyst, Security Manager, Security Auditor, Security Architect, Security Consultant, and Network Architect.

Requires a minimum of 5 years of cumulative, full-time experience in two or more of the eight CISSP domains (one year may be waived with a relevant post-secondary degree or an approved ISC2 credential); candidates lacking the full experience can pass the exam and hold the Associate of ISC2 designation while accumulating the required experience. ANSI/ANAB accredited to ISO/IEC 17024.
