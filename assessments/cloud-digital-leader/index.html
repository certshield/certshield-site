Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for anyone who wants to demonstrate their knowledge of cloud computing basics and how Google Cloud products and services can be used to achieve an organization's goals — able to articulate the capabilities of Google Cloud's core products and services and describe common business use cases. No prior technical experience is required, though experience collaborating with technical professionals is recommended.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, and French in addition to English — the broadest language availability of any Google Cloud certification on this site.
