About the Official AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate Exam

Sourced directly from the official AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate Exam Guide, published by Amazon Web Services (AWS) — Live version effective August 30, 2022.

Questions
65 total questions (50 count toward the score; 15 are unscored pretest items used by AWS to evaluate future exam content and are not identified to the candidate)
Time Limit
130 minutes (2 hours 10 minutes)
Passing Score
720 on a scaled 100–1,000 range (compensatory scoring across the whole exam; no minimum is required in any individual domain)
Registration Fee
$150 USD (holders of an active AWS certification may be eligible for a 50% discount voucher)
Delivery
Pearson VUE — available both at an authorized testing center and via online proctoring
Validity
3-year certification cycle; recertify by passing the current version of this exam, or by earning AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Professional, before expiration

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Design Secure Architectures 30%
  • Design Resilient Architectures 26%
  • Design High-Performing Architectures 24%
  • Design Cost-Optimized Architectures 20%

Who this certification is for:

AWS recommends at least 1 year of hands-on experience designing cloud solutions that use AWS services; AWS notes that candidates with 1–3 years of general IT experience have also successfully prepared for and earned this certification as a starting point, and suggests AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner first for those without prior IT experience. There is no formal enrollment prerequisite.

The exam does not require deep hands-on coding experience, although familiarity with basic programming concepts is considered an advantage. Version 1.1 of the official exam guide is the current revision; the SAA-C03 exam version itself has been live since August 30, 2022, with no successor exam code announced by AWS as of this validation date.
