About the Official ISSEP Information Systems Security Engineering Exam

Sourced directly from the official ISSEP Information Systems Security Engineering Exam Guide, published by ISC2 — Live version effective August 1, 2025.

Questions

Validity
3-year certification cycle. Candidates who hold an active CISSP earn 120 CPE credits across the shared CISSP cycle, of which 20 Group A credits must be ISSEP-specific (automatically counted toward the CISSP requirement, not earned separately); a single $135 annual maintenance fee covers any combination of ISC2 certifications held, including ISSEP, regardless of how many are held simultaneously

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Systems Security Engineering Foundations ~24%
  • Risk Management ~20%
  • Security Planning and Engineering ~22%

  • Secure Operations, Change Management and Disposal ~14%

Who this certification is for:

ISC2 describes the ISSEP as a security leader who specializes in the practical application of systems engineering principles and processes to develop secure systems — analyzing organizational needs, defining security requirements, designing security architectures, developing secure designs, implementing system security, and supporting system security assessment and authorization for government and industry.

Requires either an active CISSP credential in good standing plus 2 years of cumulative, full-time experience in one or more of the five ISSEP domains, or a minimum of 7 years of cumulative, full-time experience across two or more domains (one year may be waived with a relevant post-secondary degree or an approved ISC2 credential; part-time work and internships may also count). ANAB-accredited to ISO/IEC 17024. ISC2 periodically updates the exam via a formal Job Task Analysis (JTA) to keep content aligned with current practitioner roles and responsibilities.
