Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for experienced developers and architects who design and manage autonomous, AI-driven agentic workflows — with deep experience using large language models, applying agent design patterns, writing code, and integrating data sources on Google Cloud — typically with 3+ years of hands-on cloud experience, including 1+ years building agentic solutions on Google Cloud.

This is a two-part certification: candidates must pass both this proctored multiple-choice exam and a separate hands-on skills labs component (hosted on Google Skills, estimated under 5 hours, taken only after passing the multiple-choice exam) to earn the credential. English only; no formal prerequisites. Beta window: September 8–30, 2026; general availability begins mid-November 2026.
