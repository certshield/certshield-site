Who this certification is for:

Salesforce recommends approximately 1 year of experience with Salesforce platform configuration and standard objects, including Data 360, plus hands-on experience building agents in Agent Builder and creating prompt templates in Prompt Builder. There is no formal prerequisite, though Salesforce suggests candidates may benefit from first holding Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator and Salesforce Certified Platform App Builder.

This exam's content domains and weights reflect the Spring '26-aligned exam guide; several third-party study resources published before this update still describe an earlier domain structure (for example, "Agentforce Concepts," "Agent Design," or "Agentforce and Data Cloud" as separate domains) that no longer matches the live, current exam guide on help.salesforce.com. This file is validated against the current domain structure only.
