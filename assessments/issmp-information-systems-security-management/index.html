3-year certification cycle. Candidates who hold an active CISSP must earn 120 CPE credits across the shared CISSP cycle, of which 20 Group A credits must be ISSMP-specific (these are automatically counted toward the CISSP requirement, not earned separately); a single $135 annual maintenance fee covers any combination of ISC2 certifications held, including ISSMP, regardless of how many are held simultaneously

Who this certification is for:

ISC2 describes the ISSMP as a security leader who specializes in establishing, presenting, and governing information security programs and who demonstrates management and leadership skills — directing the alignment of security programs with the organization's mission, goals, and strategies to meet enterprise financial and operational requirements in support of its desired risk position.

Requires either an active CISSP credential in good standing plus 2 years of cumulative, full-time experience in one or more of the six ISSMP domains, or a minimum of 7 years of cumulative, full-time experience across two or more domains (a relevant post-secondary degree or an approved ISC2 credential may satisfy one year of the requirement; part-time work and internships may also count). ANAB-accredited to ISO/IEC 17024. ISC2 periodically updates the exam via a formal Job Task Analysis (JTA) to keep content aligned with current practitioner roles and responsibilities.
