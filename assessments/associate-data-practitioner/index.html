Questions

2 hours

Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

  • Data Preparation and Ingestion ~30%
  • Data Analysis and Presentation ~27%
  • Data Pipeline Orchestration ~18%
  • Data Management ~25%

Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for individuals who secure and manage data on Google Cloud, with experience using Google Cloud data services for data ingestion, transformation, pipeline management, analysis, machine learning, and visualization, and a basic understanding of cloud computing concepts (IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS). Recommended experience: 6+ months of hands-on experience working with data on Google Cloud.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
