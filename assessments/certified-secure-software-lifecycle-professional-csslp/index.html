Who this certification is for:

ISC2 describes the CSSLP as validating that software professionals have the expertise to incorporate security practices — authentication, authorization, and auditing — into each phase of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), from design and implementation through testing and deployment; the typical audience includes software architects, engineers, developers, application security specialists, QA testers, penetration testers, and project managers.

Requires a minimum of 4 years of cumulative, paid SDLC professional work experience across one or more of the eight CSSLP domains, or 3 years of such experience combined with a four-year degree (or regional equivalent) in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. Candidates who lack the required experience may still sit and pass the exam to become an Associate of ISC2, with 5 years to earn the required experience. ANSI/ISO/IEC 17024-accredited. ISC2 periodically updates the exam via a formal Job Task Analysis (JTA) to keep content aligned with current practitioner roles and responsibilities.
