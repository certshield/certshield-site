Official exam domains this diagnostic mirrors:

Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for database professionals with experience designing, creating, managing, and troubleshooting Google Cloud databases used by applications to store and retrieve data — comfortable translating business and technical requirements into scalable, resilient, and cost-effective database solutions. Recommended experience: 5+ years of overall database and IT experience, including 2+ years of hands-on experience with Google Cloud database solutions.

No formal prerequisites. Also offered in Japanese in addition to English.
