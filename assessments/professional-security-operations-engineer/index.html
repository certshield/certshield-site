Who this certification is for:

Google Cloud's own certification exam guide describes this credential for practitioners who detect, monitor, analyze, investigate, and respond to security threats against workloads, endpoints, and infrastructure — proficient in writing detection rules, log prioritization and ingestion, orchestration, and response automation, with experience leveraging posture and threat intelligence for detection and response — typically with 3+ years of security industry experience, including 1+ years using Google Cloud security tooling.

No formal prerequisites; also offered in Japanese in addition to English. The exam assesses knowledge of both Google Security Operations (SecOps) and Security Command Center (SCC).
