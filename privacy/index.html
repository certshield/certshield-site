Your data

Privacy Policy

What CertShield Practice stores, what it does not collect, and how optional analytics works. Last updated 3 November 2026.

The short version

CertShield Practice has no accounts and no sign-up for the mock exams; the only two forms are the optional Feedback box and the optional free-seat email alerts described below. The diagnostics run in your browser: your answers, confidence ratings and progress stay on your device and are never sent to us. Google Analytics, the only tracking tool on the site, loads only if you choose “Allow analytics”.

What is stored in your browser

  • Practice progress: your answers, flags and place in each assessment so you can resume where you stopped, plus a short history of your earlier attempts so you can compare them.
  • An unsent feedback draft: if you close the Feedback box before sending, your text is kept for this browser tab only (session storage) and is deleted when you send it or close the tab.
  • Your analytics choice: whether you chose “Allow analytics” or “No thanks”.

This is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clear it in your browser settings at any time; the start-over option inside an assessment clears that assessment's progress.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose “Allow analytics”, we load Google Analytics 4. It records standard visit data (pages viewed, links clicked, referring site, approximate location, device and browser type), which we use to understand which pages and links are useful. It never receives your diagnostic answers, confidence ratings or results. Google acts as our service provider; see Google's privacy policy.

If you choose “No thanks”, nothing from Google is loaded. You can change your mind at any time with Analytics settings in the footer; choosing “No thanks” stops the tag and removes its cookies.

Links to Udemy and other sites

The full-length practice exams are CertShield's own courses, sold and delivered by Udemy. Course buttons and offer links on this site carry CertShield's instructor referral or coupon code, so Udemy credits CertShield when you enrol, at no extra cost to you. Udemy's own privacy policy applies once you leave this site, as do the policies of the official vendor documentation sites we link to for further reading.

Free-seat email alerts (optional)

If you type your email address into a “Notify me” box (in the footer, on the home page or on the Offers page) and tick the consent box, we use it only to email you when a new round of free seats opens. The box passes your address to Brevo, our email platform, which sends you a confirmation message first; you are only on the list after you confirm. Brevo processes your data under its privacy policy, and the box checks that you are a person with Cloudflare Turnstile, which loads only when you click into the box. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe at certshield.academy. Your browser remembers that you signed up (a flag in local storage) so the boxes stop asking.

Feedback box (optional)

The Feedback button lets you write to us. Nothing is sent until you press “Send feedback”. We then receive what you wrote, the address of the page (and, in a quiz, the question you were on), your browser's language and, only if you add it, your email address so we can reply. The message travels over an encrypted connection to certshield.academy, which passes it to our email provider, Brevo, for delivery to contact@certshield.academy. A Cloudflare Turnstile check, loaded only when you open the box (never on page load), confirms you are not a bot; it is covered by Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We keep no copy on this website and use the message only to improve the site and to reply to you. Please do not include personal details you would not want us to see.

Children

This site is a study aid for certification candidates and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights and contact

Apart from feedback you choose to send us and the email address you choose to give for free-seat alerts (held by Brevo, removable with the unsubscribe link), we hold no personal data about you on this site, so there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete here. To have a feedback message deleted, or for any other privacy question, including about data in Google Analytics if you allowed it, email contact@certshield.academy.

The same principles apply on certshield.academy. This page is written in plain language and describes how the site works today; it is not legal advice.
