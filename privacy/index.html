Privacy Policy

What CertShield Practice stores, what it does not collect, and how optional analytics works. Last updated 10 October 2026.

The short version

CertShield Practice has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that collect personal data. The diagnostics run in your browser: your answers, confidence ratings and progress stay on your device and are never sent to us. Google Analytics, the only tracking tool on the site, loads only if you choose “Allow analytics”.

What is stored in your browser

  • Practice progress: your answers, flags and place in each assessment so you can resume where you stopped, plus a short history of your earlier attempts so you can compare them.
  • Your analytics choice: whether you chose “Allow analytics” or “No thanks”.

This is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clear it in your browser settings at any time; the start-over option inside an assessment clears that assessment's progress.

Analytics (optional)

If you choose “Allow analytics”, we load Google Analytics 4. It records standard visit data (pages viewed, links clicked, referring site, approximate location, device and browser type), which we use to understand which pages and links are useful. It never receives your diagnostic answers, confidence ratings or results. Google acts as our service provider; see Google's privacy policy.

If you choose “No thanks”, nothing from Google is loaded. You can change your mind at any time with Analytics settings in the footer; choosing “No thanks” stops the tag and removes its cookies.

Links to Udemy and other sites

The full-length practice exams are CertShield's own courses, sold and delivered by Udemy. Course buttons and offer links on this site carry CertShield's instructor referral or coupon code, so Udemy credits CertShield when you enrol, at no extra cost to you. Udemy's own privacy policy applies once you leave this site, as do the policies of the official vendor documentation sites we link to for further reading.

Children

This site is a study aid for certification candidates and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about you on this site, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete here. For any privacy question, including about data in Google Analytics if you allowed it, email contact@certshield.academy.

The same principles apply on certshield.academy. This page is written in plain language and describes how the site works today; it is not legal advice.
