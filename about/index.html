About CertShield Practice

CertShield Practice offers free certification mock exams. Here is who builds them and why.

CertShield Practice exists to answer one question before you commit to a full practice course: "Am I actually ready?" Each free 30+ question diagnostic gives an evidence-based, domain-level readiness signal with fully explained reasoning — original content, technically validated against current official certification documentation.

Who Built This

CertShield Practice is built and maintained by Priya Dwivedi (referred to as Priya D elsewhere on this site), an independent certification practice-exam architect. Her practice exams are research-driven and scenario-based, covering fast-growing certification areas including AI/ML, Agentic AI, Cloud Architecture, Data Engineering, Security, Governance and Automation — built for professionals who want structured preparation and a realistic exam experience, not a generic question bank.

Alongside building exams, she writes and researches content on certification trends and exam-preparation strategy in these same areas — the same research process feeds both her writing and every question she publishes.

Every question in the full-length practice exams — the same standard this free diagnostic follows — is technically validated against current official documentation, then peer-reviewed by subject-matter experts before publication, so no question ships on one person's judgment alone.

As of October 2026, her Udemy practice exams have reached 12,348 students across 43 courses, with 1,530 learner reviews — verifiable directly on her Udemy instructor profile, LinkedIn, or YouTube.

The full-length practice exams themselves are hosted on Udemy; certshield.academy has further details on each certification's coverage. This diagnostic site is independent of, and not affiliated with, any certifying body named on it.
