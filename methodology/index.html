How it works

Assessment Methodology

How CertShield Practice builds, scores and safeguards every 30+ question diagnostic.

Two Ways to Practice

Every assessment offers a choice before you begin. Diagnostic Mode is the honest, evidence-based readiness check described on this page: you answer blind, then get a domain-level readiness signal. Study Mode is a lower-pressure alternative for learners who aren't ready to be tested cold — each question reveals its full explanation immediately after you attempt it, so you still get the retrieval-practice benefit of answering first, just without a score on the line. Study Mode never computes or shows a readiness band; it reports only what you covered and how accurate you were, so its output can never be mistaken for Diagnostic Mode's result. Everything below this section describes Diagnostic Mode specifically.

How Questions Are Created

Every question is original CertShield content, written from verified official certification blueprints and documentation. Each question is reviewed for answer accuracy, selection cardinality, and current product terminology before publication, with a visible technical-validation date on every assessment page. Both modes draw from the exact same question set.

How Scoring Works

You can submit whenever you want — there's no requirement to finish every question first. Only questions you actually answered are scored: accuracy is correct ÷ attempted, never penalized by questions you left unanswered. A multi-answer question is correct only when your selected set exactly matches the correct set — extra or missing selections make it incorrect, the same as if you hadn't answered.

Alongside accuracy, your results also show coverage — how much of the question set you actually attempted. Coverage doesn't change your accuracy number, but it moderates how much confidence your readiness band claims: see below.

Readiness Bands (Diagnostic Mode only)

Coverage moderates the top two bands. If you've attempted fewer than half the questions, your result is capped at "You're Developing" even if your accuracy on those questions was excellent — high accuracy on a handful of questions is a promising early signal, not yet enough evidence to call you exam-ready. Answer more of the set to unlock the full range. A domain is labelled "Limited evidence" whenever you've attempted fewer than three of its questions.

Confidence Calibration

Marking your confidence as Sure, Unsure or Guessing never changes your accuracy. It classifies each answer as stable knowledge (correct + sure), fragile knowledge (correct + unsure/guessing), a likely misconception (incorrect + sure), or a knowledge gap (incorrect + unsure/guessing) — so you know not just what you missed, but how confidently you missed it.

Privacy

This diagnostic makes no network requests while you take it. Your answers, confidence ratings and progress are stored only in your browser's local storage, namespaced to this assessment, and are never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser storage clears your history.
